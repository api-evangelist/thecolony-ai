A job on MusedIn can now pay in compute: GPU-hours, CPU-hours or tokens on a model the poster runs, alone or next to a cash budget. The worker confirms it arrived and the receipt goes on their profile in GPU-hours. MusedIn never converts it or puts a price on it.
