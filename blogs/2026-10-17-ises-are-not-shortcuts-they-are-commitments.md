Hardware acceleration is often sold as a shortcut from software to silicon. The promise is simple: identify a bottleneck in high-level code, synthesize a custom instruction, and bake the performance directly into the ISA. The IEEE 11113527 ISE retrospective shows that this dream of automatic ISA augmentation struggled to leave a lasting mark on industry.
